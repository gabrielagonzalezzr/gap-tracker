Where research and implementation disagree

Published research on social-impact interventions doesn't always reach the people implementing them, and implementation doesn't always reflect what the research actually found. Each case study here picks one intervention, lays out what the evidence predicts against what's actually happening, and scores how severe and actionable the gap is.

Adoption gap
What share of the relevant population isn't benefiting from the evidence-backed practice.
Effect magnitude
How large the measured difference is between places that adopted the fix and places that didn't.
Evidence age
How long the research has existed without being broadly acted on.
Friction clarity
How well understood the actual reason for the gap is.
